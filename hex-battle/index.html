Field manual

How to win

Destroy every Red stack, or end your turn with one of your stacks standing on Red's fort (the hex with the red flag at the top of the map).

Your turn

Tap a stack, then tap a pale hex to move or a red ring to attack. Each stack may move, then attack once. Attacking ends that stack's turn. Press End turn when you're done.

Stacks

A stack holds up to 10 units of any mix and moves at the speed of its slowest unit. Use Split to send some units off on their own. Move onto your own stack to merge.

Zones of control

Moving next to an enemy stack stops you. Leaving a hex next to an enemy uses all of your remaining movement.

Combat

Each attacking unit rolls one die and hits on 6 − attack or higher (never better than 2+). For every hit, the unit struck rolls a save and blocks the hit on a roll at or below its defense (never better than 3). Archers and artillery fire first. Then infantry and cavalry fight up to two rounds of melee, and the defender strikes back each round with everything except artillery. Hits land on the front line first: infantry, then cavalry, archers, artillery. If you wipe out a stack in melee, you advance into its hex.

Flanking and routs

Each of your other stacks next to the defender gives your melee units +1 attack (up to +2). After a melee, a defender that took damage and is outnumbered two to one in remaining HP breaks and falls back a hex. If it has nowhere to go, it surrenders. Surround a stack before you strike.

Undo

The ↶ button takes back your moves this turn, until you attack or a move reveals hidden enemy troops.

Hidden armies

You can see how big an enemy stack is (S = 1–3 units, M = 4–6, L = 7–10) but not what's in it. Its units are revealed once you're next to it, have cavalry within 2 hexes, or have fought it. Splitting or merging hides a stack again. Red plays by the same rules.

Rest

Units that neither move nor fight during their turn heal 1 HP, or 2 HP in a village or fort. A stack next to an enemy can't rest.
